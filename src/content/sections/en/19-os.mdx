## Free operating systems

The keystone. If client-side scanning can be imposed by the operating system itself, the only real counter is to control that OS. That's true on the phone (GrapheneOS) and on the computer (Linux). Don't underestimate this: as long as you stay on Windows, macOS or a Google Android, you don't truly control your machine.

#### iPhone: Lockdown Mode

On iPhone, iPad and Mac, Apple offers **Lockdown Mode**, designed for people who may be targeted by sophisticated spyware. It blocks most attachments in Messages, FaceTime calls from people you haven't called recently, some complex web technologies, wired connections while the device is locked, and the installation of configuration profiles. Everyday use becomes less convenient: that is the price of a smaller attack surface. Turn it on in Settings > Privacy & Security > Lockdown Mode, separately on each device, after installing the latest update.

#### Harden your device

Even without switching OS, you gain a lot: install apps via **F-Droid** (free store) or **Aurora Store**, replace Google services with **microG**, create separate profiles, cut needless network permissions, disable automatic cloud backup and the advertising ID.
