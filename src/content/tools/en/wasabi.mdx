  <Fragment slot="tags">
    <span class="tool-tag">🔴 Advanced</span>
    <span class="tool-tag">CoinJoin</span>
    <span class="tool-tag">🌍 · open-source</span>
  </Fragment>
  <Fragment slot="what">
    A free Bitcoin wallet with built-in <b>CoinJoin</b>: your coins are combined with other users'
    coins in a joint transaction, which breaks the visible on-chain link between origin and
    destination.
  </Fragment>
  <Fragment slot="why">
    The Bitcoin ledger is public: without care, every payment can be linked to earlier ones.
    CoinJoin restores part of that privacy without handing your funds to anyone, and the wallet runs
    over Tor.
  </Fragment>
  <Fragment slot="who">
    Experienced Bitcoin users. Since the original coordinator shut down in June 2024, you must pick
    an independent coordinator, which charges fees.
  </Fragment>
  <div class="box warn" slot="extra">
    <span class="lab">Mixed funds and platforms</span>
    <p>
      Regulated platforms may refuse or freeze funds that went through a CoinJoin. Keep a record of
      the legitimate origin of your funds.
    </p>
  </div>
  <ol slot="install">
    <li>
      Download Wasabi from <b>wasabiwallet.io</b> and verify the signature.
    </li>
    <li>In the settings, choose an independent coordinator.</li>
    <li>Turn on automatic CoinJoin, and never mix mixed and unmixed coins in the same payment.</li>
  </ol>
