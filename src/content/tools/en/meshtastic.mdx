<ToolCard tool="t-meshtastic" variant={props.variant}>
  <Fragment slot="tags">
    <span class="tool-tag">🔴 Advanced</span>
    <span class="tool-tag">Off-grid radio</span>
    <span class="tool-tag">🌍 · open-source</span>
  </Fragment>
  <Fragment slot="what">
    Text messages carried by <b>LoRa radio</b>, from device to device, with no mobile network or
    internet. Your phone connects to the device over Bluetooth.
  </Fragment>
  <Fragment slot="why">
    No operator, no server: nothing to cut off or compel. Several kilometres of range, and a mesh
    that relays messages.
  </Fragment>
  <Fragment slot="who">
    Hiking, dead zones, outages and crises. You need hardware (one radio device per person) and must
    accept short, slow messages.
  </Fragment>
  <div class="box warn" slot="extra">
    <span class="lab">Limits to know</span>
    <p>
      The default channel can be read by anyone. Direct messages are encrypted (firmware 2.5 and
      later), but radio metadata stays visible. Meshtastic does not talk to MeshCore, a competing
      project.
    </p>
  </div>
  <ol slot="install">
    <li>
      Buy a supported device (list on <b>meshtastic.org</b>) and install the app.
    </li>
    <li>Select your country's radio region: allowed frequencies and transmit time vary.</li>
    <li>
      Create a private channel with a random key: the default public channel uses a key everyone
      knows.
    </li>
  </ol>
</ToolCard>
