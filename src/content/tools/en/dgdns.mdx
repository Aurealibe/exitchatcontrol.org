  <Fragment slot="what">
    A small encrypted DNS resolver run by the Digital Society Switzerland, a non-profit
    civil-liberties association.
  </Fragment>
  <Fragment slot="why">
    It keeps no logs of IP addresses or queried domains, and filters nothing. It runs on its own
    hardware in Switzerland, publishes its production configuration on GitHub (built on dnsdist and
    Unbound) and a yearly transparency report. Being small and outside the big providers, it is a
    good second resolver, or a first one where Quad9 is no longer available.
  </Fragment>
  <Fragment slot="who">
    Everyone, especially in France from 24 November 2026. Volunteer-run with no availability
    guarantee: keep a second resolver in mind.
  </Fragment>
  <ol slot="install">
    <li>
      On Android: Settings &gt; Network &gt; <b>Private DNS</b> &gt; enter{' '}
      <b>dns.digitale-gesellschaft.ch</b>.
    </li>
    <li>
      Elsewhere (browser, PC, iPhone): set DNS-over-HTTPS to{' '}
      <b>{'https://dns.digitale-gesellschaft.ch/dns-query'}</b>. There is no unencrypted service on
      port 53, on purpose.
    </li>
  </ol>
