## VPN: the truth

A VPN is useful, but sold with a lot of lies. Here's what it actually protects.

<div class="box warn">
  <span class="lab">What a VPN does NOT do</span>
  <p>
    A VPN <strong>does not protect you from Chat Control's client-side scanning</strong>: that reads
    your messages on your device, before encryption and therefore before the VPN. It's also not an
    invisibility cloak: your OS and apps see more than your VPN provider does.
  </p>
</div>

<div class="box ok">
  <span class="lab">What a VPN does well</span>
  <p>
    It hides your activity from your <strong>ISP</strong> and masks your <strong>IP address</strong>{' '}
    (and thus location) from the sites you visit. Useful against network surveillance, geolocation
    and censorship. One piece of the puzzle, not the solution.
  </p>
</div>

<ToolCard tool="t-nym">
  <Fragment slot="tags">
    <span class="tool-tag">🟡🔴 Interm. / Advanced</span>
    <span class="tool-tag">Mixnet</span>
    <span class="tool-tag">🇨🇭 · open-source</span>
  </Fragment>
  <Fragment slot="what">
    A VPN with two modes: a fast mode (2 servers, WireGuard) and an <b>anonymous</b> mode that
    routes traffic through a 5-node <b>mixnet</b>, mixed with cover traffic.
  </Fragment>
  <Fragment slot="why">
    The mixnet blurs metadata (who talks to whom, and when), even against an observer watching the
    whole network, which a classic VPN does not do. Decentralised server network, open-source apps
    (GPL), audited by Cure53. Payment can be unlinked from usage through anonymous "zk-nym"
    credentials.
  </Fragment>
  <Fragment slot="who">
    Those who want to go beyond a classic VPN against traffic analysis, and accept an anonymous mode
    that is much slower and unsuited to streaming and downloads. Paid subscription, with a free
    trial.
  </Fragment>
  <div class="box" slot="extra">
    <span class="lab">Good to know</span>
    <p>
      The Nym network pays node operators with a token (NYM). You don't need to hold any to use the
      VPN.
    </p>
  </div>
  <ol slot="install">
    <li>
      Install NymVPN from <b>nym.com</b> (desktop and mobile).
    </li>
    <li>
      Use the <b>anonymous</b> mode for messaging and sensitive browsing, the <b>fast</b> mode for
      the rest.
    </li>
    <li>
      To keep your identity unlinked from your usage, pay in cryptocurrency or with zk-nym
      credentials.
