## Financial sovereignty

Money is surveilled and censorable too. Activists' accounts have been frozen, donations blocked, and every card payment is tracked. Financial privacy is part of sovereignty.

<div class="box honest">
  <span class="lab">On-chain privacy: what the law says</span>
  <ul>
    <li>
      <b>Swaps without an intermediary</b>: since August 2026, THORChain lets you swap bitcoin
      directly for Monero, with no account or platform. The service is new: a wrong return address
      can lose the funds.
    </li>
    <li>
      <b>Tornado Cash</b> (Ethereum): removed from US sanctions in March 2025, but its developers
      were prosecuted, with a money-laundering conviction in the Netherlands in 2024 (appeal
      pending) and a partial conviction in the United States in August 2025. The legal status of
      mixers remains uncertain in Europe.
    </li>
    <li>
      <b>July 2027</b>: the EU anti-money-laundering regulation (AMLR) bars regulated platforms from
      keeping anonymous accounts and from offering privacy-enhancing cryptocurrencies such as
      Monero. Holding in self-custody is not banned.
    </li>
  </ul>
</div>

<div class="box warn">
  <span class="lab">Legality &amp; caution</span>
  <p>
    Financial privacy is <strong>legal</strong>. Don't confuse it with tax evasion: declare what
    must be declared. Cryptocurrencies are <strong>volatile</strong> and scams are common; never
    risk what you can't lose, and learn before you act.
  </p>
</div>
